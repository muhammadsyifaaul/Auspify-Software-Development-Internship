# Task 4: Employee Management System (StaffPulse Pro)

## 📌 Overview
StaffPulse Pro is an enterprise-grade Employee Management & HR Organization Suite created for the **Auspify Technologies Software Development Internship**. It offers comprehensive personnel directory management, department budget & headcount allocation, payroll analytics, and report export.

---

## 🚀 Features Implemented
- **Employee Directory & Profile Management**:
  - Full CRUD operations: Code, Full Name, Corporate Email, Phone, Department, Role, Monthly Salary, Employment Type, Status, Date Joined.
  - Interactive Employee Profile Card drawer with quick contact and role summary.
- **Department Management & Organization Analytics**:
  - Headcount distribution across Engineering, Product & Design, HR, Marketing & Sales, Finance & Accounting.
  - Department budget vs actual salary expenditure breakdown.
- **Search & Multi-criteria Filtering**:
  - Real-time search across Name, Employee Code, Role, and Email.
  - Filter by Department and Employment Type (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Internship).
- **Executive Payroll & KPI Dashboard**:
  - Total workforce headcount.
  - Real-time Total Monthly Payroll calculation.
  - Average Salary index per employee.
  - Active department counter.
- **Data Export & Persistence**:
  - One-click CSV Payroll Export.
  - Persistent state in browser `localStorage`.
